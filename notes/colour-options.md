## Recommendation

**Electric blue** is the best fit for the existing video and exterior lighting photographs. The cool blue works with the evening sky and separates the electrical service identity from the warm wood and interior finishes in the footage. Use the blue for the quote button, active links, icons, and a few headline details. Keep most content on white or pale blue-grey and the services section in midnight blue. The preview uses a pale quick-link strip with blue details to keep the accent from dominating the page.

**Carbon red** gives the strongest industrial feel. Use the dark red for buttons and a few key signals, and a lighter red tint for small text against the dark base. It can suggest urgency, so avoid making every section red.

**Deep green** is calmer and distinctive. It may lead visitors to assume a sustainability or EV focus that is only one part of the current service offering.

The previous design put yellow across the whole quick-link strip, the primary action, and several headings. The selected design uses a pale quick-link strip and keeps the electric blue concentrated on important actions and details. Nielsen Norman Group recommends using the accent to highlight priority actions and applying it consistently.

The contrast figures above are calculated from the specified solid-colour text and button backgrounds using the WCAG formula. WCAG 2.2 AA requires at least 4.5:1 for ordinary text and 3:1 for large text. Text over moving footage needs a separate visual check because the background varies by frame.

## Research references

- [Nielsen Norman Group: Using Color to Enhance Your Design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/color-enhance-design/) — reserve accent colour for visual hierarchy and primary actions.
- [W3C: WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag/) and [Understanding non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast) — text and interface contrast requirements.
- [ABB branding tips](https://library.e.abb.com/public/28d1a8bbae384ff6968021b8f685fb01/ABB%20branding%20tips%202022.pdf) — an electrical brand using red strategically with neutral space.
- [Eaton channel partner graphic guidelines](https://www.eaton.com/content/dam/eaton/company/policies-and-statements/eaton-channel-partner-graphic-guidelines.pdf) — Eaton Blue, black, and white.
- [Schneider Electric catalogue](https://download.se.com/files?p_Doc_Ref=CA909013E&p_File_Name=CA909013E.pdf&p_enDocType=Catalog) — a green electrical brand direction.

The proposed hex values are original concepts for Python Electric, not copied from those companies.
